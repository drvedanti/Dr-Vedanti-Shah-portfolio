India 2050 · Care Capacity Intelligence

Use incomplete evidence without pretending it is complete.

KAALJI is a proposed decision-support product for people trying to understand care capacity. It does not declare shortages. It helps separate what the evidence shows, what it cannot support, and what should be investigated next.

Prototype v2 · proposed, not validated
The product question
What do we actually know about care capacity here?

The hardest part is often not finding another number. It is knowing whether two numbers are comparable, what they really measure, and which missing evidence would change the decision.

Interactive investigation

Start with the question, not the dashboard.

This demo uses a small, source-traceable evidence set from the India 2050 project. The interaction is illustrative: it demonstrates product logic rather than live national monitoring.

Try a question
Portfolio-safe by design. This prototype sends no text or data to a server, uses no third-party analytics, and contains only public/illustrative evidence. A production version would require authentication, access controls, audit logs and governed source ingestion.

    Recommended next evidence

    Obtain cohort- and date-aligned registration, employment and active-availability data before deciding whether the constraint is workforce supply, conversion, access, or measurement.

    Responsibility boundaries

    AI can help navigate the evidence. It should not quietly redefine it.

    The prototype uses AI only where language and exploration benefit from it; evidence checks remain inspectable and consequential judgment remains human.

    AI helps with language

    Useful for search, synthesis and exploration.

    • Find and summarize relevant evidence
    • Explain source-supported signals
    • Surface candidate hypotheses
    • Translate natural-language questions into retrieval

    Evidence rules stay explicit

    Reproducible checks should not depend on generative judgment.

    • Source provenance and metadata
    • Compatibility checks
    • Calculations and scenario math
    • Evidence labels and audit trail

    People own consequential decisions

    The product supports investigation; it does not replace accountable judgment.

    • Interpret competing explanations
    • Choose what evidence to pursue
    • Make programme or policy decisions
    • Make any clinical or patient-level decision
    Proof of work

    Built from a traceable research and product chain.

    This demo is one layer of a larger portfolio project: evidence register → analytical workbook → care-capacity framework → decision log → product requirements → prototype → validation plan.
