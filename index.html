The transition

Clinical experience gave me proximity to problems. Product thinking gives me a way to work on them.

My transition into product didn't begin with a product job. It began with seeing healthcare up close — the friction, workarounds, handoffs and human details that are easy to miss from a distance.

Now I'm deliberately building the second half of that perspective: product thinking, technology, AI, data, business and the discipline of turning observations into things people can actually use.
