Care-capacity decision support

Find where care capacity breaks before choosing the intervention.

KAALJI is a proposed tool for programme, workforce and strategy teams planning elder-care services. It helps test whether the problem is really training — or whether capacity is being lost after people are trained.

Evidence firstDecision orientedProposed · not validated
The practical question Before we train more caregivers, do we know that training is the bottleneck?

If the real constraint is workforce activation, geography, availability, access or retention, more training alone may not create more care.

KAALJI recommendation

Should we invest in more caregiver training?

The system has evidence of training activity, but not enough evidence to show that training is the main constraint.

DO NOT JUMP TO TRAINING YET
Current conclusion Training may be part of the answer. The current evidence cannot show that it is the bottleneck.

We can see that people were trained. We cannot yet see how many became active caregivers, where they are available, or how much care they delivered.

Why this matters

If capacity is being lost after training, expanding training first could spend resources without fixing the real constraint.

Where does the evidence become uncertain?

The pathway makes the missing conversion visible. A stage can be relevant without being equivalent to the next one.
